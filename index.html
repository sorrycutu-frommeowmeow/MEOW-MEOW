A letter for Cutu

Dear Cutu,

I'm sorry.

You came to me excited, ready to tell me about the function and your day. And instead of asking how it went, I asked you for photos.

I understand now why that hurt. It wasn't really about the photos. It was about the attention and care you deserved in that moment. You wanted to share something that made you happy, and I made you feel unheard, like what you had to say mattered less than what I wanted.

I should have asked first. How was your day? What was the best part? Who did you see? And then I should have just listened, and let you tell it your way, at your pace.

Your feelings, your stories and your happiness matter to me. I'm not writing this to make everything okay again. You don't owe me forgiveness, or a reply, or anything else right now. Take all the time you need.

I want to do better. Not just with sweet words, but with what I actually do: listening more, asking more, and giving you my full attention.

With love,
Your Meow Meow 🐾
